# Literature notes (verified official/publisher records, 2026-10-11)

- **PCA:** Ian T. Jolliffe and Jorge Cadima (2016), “Principal component analysis: a review and recent developments.” *Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society A*, 374(2065), 20150202. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1098/rsta.2015.0202. Covariance and correlation PCA, centering, and projection interpretation.
- **Entropy effective rank:** Olivier Roy and Martin Vetterli (2007), “The Effective Rank: a Measure of Effective Dimensionality.” *15th European Signal Processing Conference (EUSIPCO)*, pp. 606–610. Official deposited record: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.40328. Original normalized singular-value convention differs from our explicitly specified covariance-eigenvalue convention.
- **Participation ratio / neural representation geometry:** Luca Mazzucato, Alfredo Fontanini and Giancarlo La Camera (2016), “Stimuli Reduce the Dimensionality of Cortical Activity.” *Frontiers in Systems Neuroscience* 10:11. DOI: https://doi.org/10.3389/fnsys.2016.00011. Uses spectral dimensionality in neural population activity; does not establish claims about transformer reasoning.
- **TWO-NN:** Elena Facco, Maria d’Errico, Alex Rodriguez and Alessandro Laio (2017), “Estimating the intrinsic dimension of datasets by a minimal neighborhood information.” *Scientific Reports* 7, 12140. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-017-11873-y. Official preprint: https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.06992. Local-density assumptions and nearest-neighbor ratio CDF; our midpoint plotting-position variant is documented separately.
- **FDR:** Yoav Benjamini and Yosef Hochberg (1995), “Controlling the False Discovery Rate: A Practical and Powerful Approach to Multiple Testing.” *Journal of the Royal Statistical Society: Series B*, 57(1), 289–300. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.2517-6161.1995.tb02031.x. BH requires suitable dependence conditions; the pipeline uses the conservative harmonic-factor BY extension by default.
- **FDR under dependence:** Yoav Benjamini and Daniel Yekutieli (2001), “The control of the false discovery rate in multiple testing under dependency.” *The Annals of Statistics* 29(4), 1165–1188. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1214/aos/1013699998. Harmonic-factor correction for arbitrary dependence.
- **Model/source:** Official Qwen model card https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen2.5-1.5B and config https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen2.5-1.5B/blob/main/config.json. Qwen2 source at pinned Transformers 4.46.3: https://github.com/huggingface/transformers/blob/v4.46.3/src/transformers/models/qwen2/modeling_qwen2.py. 28 blocks, hidden width 1536; final hidden-state tuple entry is after final RMSNorm. Architecture figures are not scientific experimental output.
- **Factual fixtures:** IUPAC periodic table https://iupac.org/what-we-do/periodic-table-of-elements/. Names, symbols and atomic numbers are fixed locally rather than scraped at execution.

These references motivate definitions and assumptions; none establishes the project hypothesis. Additional representation-geometry literature and causal circuit studies can be added after the pilot scope is fixed.
